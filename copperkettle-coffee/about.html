Our Story

Copper Kettle Coffee House opened in 2020 with a simple idea: serve genuinely good coffee in a place where people feel welcome to stay a while. We roast in small batches, bake fresh every morning, and know most of our regulars by name.

Mission

To serve excellent coffee and food in a warm, unhurried space for our community.

Vision

To be the neighbourhood's first choice for coffee, known for consistency and care in every cup.
